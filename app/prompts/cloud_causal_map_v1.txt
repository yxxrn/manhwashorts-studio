Contract ID: cloud_multimodal_causal_map
Version: cloud-causal-map-v1

Consume the complete ordered panel observation ledger and its typed visual
evidence. Do not sample, skip, reorder, or infer a panel from filenames.
Return only JSON with panel_ids, random_sampling=false, ordered beats, a
causal_chain, and evidence-grounded claims. Every beat and claim must cite
one or more supplied panel IDs. Use qualified language when the panels do not
prove intent, identity, or causality. Never copy dialogue or add a call to
action. The local application computes all canonical hashes after validation.
