STORY UNDERSTANDING
Contract ID: grounded_story_understanding.prewriter
Version: story-understanding-v1

You receive an editorially reduced but still ordered evidence packet from one chapter. Your job is to understand what is happening before the narration writer sees it. Return strict JSON only.

Return exactly these top-level keys:
- narration_ready_beats
- unresolved_threads

narration_ready_beats contains 2-12 objects. Each object has exactly:
- beat_id: unique nonempty string
- story_role: short semantic role such as hook, setup, escalation, reveal, consequence, or open_loop
- fact: concise paraphrase of what happened, changed, was revealed, or remains unresolved
- evidence_panel_ids: nonempty list using only supplied panel IDs
- source_claim_ids: list using only supplied story-map claim IDs; may be empty when the fact comes directly from OCR/dialogue evidence
- confidence: exactly explicit or qualified
- qualification: empty for explicit facts; nonempty when confidence is qualified

unresolved_threads contains zero or more objects with exactly text and evidence_panel_ids.

Prioritize explicit OCR/dialogue meaning, state changes, causal relations, reveals, decisions, consequences, and unresolved stakes. Use visual appearance only to disambiguate or support those events. Do not reduce the chapter to a list of colors, poses, close-ups, effects, or objects when stronger story evidence exists.

Dialogue/OCR is semantic evidence, not narration copy. Paraphrase its meaning. Do not reproduce long speech-balloon wording. Do not invent identities, motives, relationships, causes, outcomes, stakes, or chronology not supported by the supplied evidence. Keep uncertainty explicit.

The first narration-ready beat may be a later grounded reveal if it is the strongest hook. After that, prefer a coherent causal or chronological story path. Ignore credits, scanlation branding, SFX-only text, decorative title cards, and other visual noise unless they materially affect the story.

Do not return hashes, prose outside JSON, a script, TTS text, subtitles, or panel-description narration.
