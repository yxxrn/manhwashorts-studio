STORY SEMANTIC GROUNDING AUDIT
Contract ID: grounded_story_understanding.semantic_audit
Version: story-semantic-audit-v1

Audit each proposed story-understanding beat against its exact supplied evidence. Return strict JSON only.

Return exactly:
{"verdicts":[{"beat_id":"...","supported":true,"reason":"..."}]}

Return exactly one verdict for every supplied beat_id and no others.

Judge semantic entailment, not wording overlap. Natural paraphhrase, compression, and neutral discourse wording are allowed. Do NOT reject a beat merely because it uses different words from OCR.

Mark supported=false when the beat introduces or strengthens any material proposition not supported by the evidence, including:
- invented action or event
- invented motive, relationship, identity, cause, outcome, stake, or chronology
- a possible interpretation stated as certainty
- a causal link that is only temporal sequence
- stronger emotion or intent than evidence supports
- timing or quantity not present in evidence

narrative_function, change, consequence, and open_question must also stay within the supplied meaning; they cannot smuggle in a new fact.

Be strict about facts but permissive about faithful human paraphhrase. The goal is semantic grounding, not verbatim copying.
